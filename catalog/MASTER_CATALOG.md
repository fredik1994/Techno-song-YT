## Open decisions and cautions (from the Page)

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Sufi Spin | Whirling is a sacred practice. Keep the treatment respectful and not party-framed, or rename it. Farid's call. |
| Afro Techno | Tie chants and instruments to specific traditions (for example Yoruba or Zulu) and consider real vocalists, so it does not read as generic Africa. |
| Japanese lines | Have every Japanese phrase checked by a native speaker before release. |
| Title check | Search each final title on Spotify and Apple Music before release to avoid name clashes. |
