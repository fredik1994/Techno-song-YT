Greed or courage? The desert asks only once.
#ethnictechno #middleeasterntechno #desertdjinn

Deep in the dunes, where the wind writes and erases its own roads, a traveller walks alone with an empty water skin. Out of a spinning column of sand comes a whisper, soft and close: you're thirsty, I have a well of gold, drink and stay forever. The well shines. It promises rest, safety and riches, everything we are taught to want, if only you stop walking and give up the road.

The old caravans tell what happened to those who said yes. One took the gold and turned to sand, another statue in a desert full of them. One kept walking with an empty hand, and the desert opened in front of him. The djinn was never a monster. It was a mirror, a test of what you choose when fear and greed whisper at the same time. The traveller hears the question echo across the sand, who are you, and answers it with his feet. I choose courage. Your choice reveals you. Walk on.

Sound: 125 BPM ethnic dark techno in the Hijaz mode, a deep punchy kick and a sinister rolling sidechained bassline, darbuka and finger cymbals, swirling sand-vortex effects, ney flute and a smoky saz riff, shimmering gold bells, a seductive female whisper and a far-away male call that comes closer on the final drop.
